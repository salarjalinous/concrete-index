The figures above are the briefing's headline readings, computed live from the City Record and the OMB capital plan. The trailing-twelve-month award figure keeps one record per procurement and, like the index on the “Analysis” page, sets aside awards of $500M and up along with renewals and extensions, so a single megaproject or a continuation of existing work does not distort the trend. That new-work basis is why it reads below the two gross figures on the site: the “Awarded, 12 mo” tile on the home page and the headline total on the “Awarded” page, both of which count every construction award, renewals and megaprojects included. The open-pipeline and pace measures come from the capital plan on the same basis as the “Pipeline” and “Funnel” pages. The narrative below reads these figures in plain language, and the note at its end cites the sources and their update cadence.
This briefing is composed automatically each time the page loads, drawing on the City Record of contract awards (updated daily) and the OMB capital plan (updated three times a year). Each figure reflects its source as of the date shown beside it. Definitions and treatment: see the methodology notes on the “Awarded”, “Pipeline”, “Funnel”, and “Agencies” pages.
