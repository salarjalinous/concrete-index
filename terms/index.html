Terms of use

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What this is

The Concrete Index is an independent personal project by Salar Jalinous. It reads public records about New York City public construction and presents them under a published methodology. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for the City of New York, New York State, the MTA, the School Construction Authority, the NYC Comptroller, or any of their agencies, and it is not affiliated with or speaking for any employer.

Provided as is

The figures are computed from public data that the publishing agencies can revise, correct, or withdraw at any time. They are provided as is, without any warranty of accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or fitness for a purpose. Check anything that matters against the source record and the issuing agency, which remains the authoritative source.

Not advice

This site is not credit, bonding, investment, or legal advice, nor procurement advice. Nothing here recommends any action about any company, contract, or security.

Named contractors

Figures shown next to a company's name are public award records, not credit information. They say nothing about a company's costs, margins, bonding capacity, financial condition, or performance. Ordering by dollars or counts is a sort, not a rating.

Sources and licenses

NYC Open Data, under the NYC OpenData Terms of Use: the City Record (award notices and solicitations), the OMB Capital Projects Dashboard, the School Construction Authority's active projects, and the Doing Business Database. Open NY, under the OPEN-NY Terms of Use: the MTA capital dashboard, MTA procurements, and State University Construction Fund contracts. Checkbook NYC, published by the Office of the NYC Comptroller under its disclaimer. U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics price series. Chart.js (MIT License). Source Serif 4, Public Sans, and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License). Every dataset, with its ID and link, is listed on Sources.

Your use

You may quote figures with attribution to The Concrete Index and the underlying source. Do not present them as official government statistics or as this site's assessment of any company. "NYC" and the City's marks belong to the City of New York; their appearance here describes a data source and implies no endorsement.

Corrections

If a figure looks wrong or a record about your company is mis-grouped, open an issue on the GitHub issues page with the page, the figure, and the source record. Verified grouping errors are fixed and noted in the methodology. Errors in the source data must be corrected by the publishing agency.
