Sources

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Every figure on the site traces to one of the datasets below. Each row names the publisher, the pages the dataset feeds, the refresh cadence the publisher states, and how this site reads it: in your browser when the page loads, or through the nightly archive job that writes a same-origin file under /data/.

Checkbook NYC

Published by the Office of the NYC Comptroller at checkbooknyc.com, under the Comptroller's disclaimer. Checkbook NYC serves no cross-origin access and answers in paged XML, so a browser cannot query it. The nightly jobs post to its API and write same-origin files; your browser never contacts it.

Spending Contract spending categories, one contract at a time, for registered construction contracts of $500,000 or more registered since 2019 that are open or closed within the past year Contracts (dated spend curve), Agencies and Analysis (payment velocity) See checkbooknyc.com Nightly archive (contract-search.json)

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Two monthly price series drive the cost-adjusted index. Each is rebased to its own 2019 average and blended 60 percent materials, 40 percent labor. The browser cannot reach api.bls.gov under this site's Content Security Policy, so the nightly archive job pulls both and writes cost-index.json. Terms: BLS copyright information.
