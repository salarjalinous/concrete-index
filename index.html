Public construction has a supply side everyone watches and a demand side almost no one does. The contractors, their bonds, and their backlogs are tracked closely. The pipeline that feeds them, every dollar the City budgets, solicits, and awards, sits in plain sight in the public record and is read by almost no one, because reading it means pulling thousands of notices and totaling them by hand.

The Concrete Index does that reading continuously. It pulls every construction award, solicitation, and capital commitment the City and State publish, totals them on the spot, and follows the same dollar from budget to award to payment. The result is a live, contractor-attributed view of where New York's public construction demand is forming, which agencies are moving it, and how fast it turns into cash.
