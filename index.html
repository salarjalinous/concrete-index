01How money moves

  1. 01The capital plan sets money aside

    Each project in the City's capital plan carries a budget years before a contract is let. Committed is what remains unspent on active projects: $107.9B in the latest plan.

    Pipeline

  2. 02Agencies award the work

    When a project is ready, an agency bids it out and publishes the award in the City Record. Award notices over the last twelve full months total $4.85B, including renewals and $500M+ awards.

    Awarded

  3. 03The Comptroller registers the contract

    A contract becomes a public obligation when the Comptroller registers it. The construction contracts Checkbook NYC lists for the current build carry $31.0B in current registered amounts.

    Contracts

  4. 04The City pays as work is done

    Payments follow progress on site. On those same contracts, $17.2B has been paid to date, counted over each contract's life.

    Funnel
