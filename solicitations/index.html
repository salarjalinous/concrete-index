Open solicitations carry no published contract value in this dataset, so they are listed by agency and due date, tagged by trade and borough where the title allows. These are open opportunities, not results. When each one is decided, the award notice names the winning contractor only, so the Awarded page shows who was awarded work, not who else bid.

About this page

SERIES NOTES

Source

Open solicitation notices from Current Solicitations on NYC Open Data (dataset 3khw-qi8f), the City Record's notice of procurement opportunities, in the two construction categories, with a due date that hasn't passed. Always confirm requirements and deadlines with the issuing agency; the contact listed on each notice is the authoritative source.

Where this sits in the funnel

A solicitation is the moment committed money (the Pipeline) reaches the market, before it becomes an award. Watching this page is watching next quarter's award flow form.
